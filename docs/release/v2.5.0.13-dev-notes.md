### What's Changed

**Texture displacement for agents** (8 new tools; 111 in the app, 115 with the bridge's). Everything the app's texture
panel does, an agent can now do through MCP: pick a texture from the library or import an image (an identical image is
reused, not copied), add, edit, reorder and remove up to eight layers on an object, paint where each applies, adjust the
unwrap, prepare the mesh, and bake it into real geometry, colour bakes included.

- **`get_texture_library`**, **`delete_user_texture`**: the built-in, imported and pack textures, by id.
- **`set_texture_displacement`** / **`get_texture_displacement`**: a layer's every setting, its projection (triplanar,
  cylindrical, spherical, unwrap, from a view, the model's own UVs, or a projection frame), and where it is painted.
- **`get_texture_unwrap`** / **`set_texture_unwrap`**: an unwrap layer's islands, seams, joins and placement.
- **`bake_texture_displacement`**: the bake off the main thread, one undo step, nothing changed if it runs past the
  call's time limit. It warns when the triangle budget cut detail or the texture is finer than the resolution, and says
  what to do instead.
- **`prepare_texture_mesh`**: remesh, subdivide and smooth before a bake.
- `paint_object`, `get_object_paint`, `clear_object_paint`, `remap_paint` and `pick_facet` work on texture layers; an
  open paint panel is closed first in every paint mode, so it can no longer write its own copy over a tool's paint.
- `get_scene_info`, `get_object_info` and `load_model` report each object's texture layers and whether it needs a bake.
  Mesh edits (repair, split, cut, merge, reload, replace) say which texture paint or layers they dropped.

**Seeing the result: `render_plate_view` reworked.**

- **Close-ups**: frame a box or a point, use an exact camera, or replay the camera of an earlier picture.
- **Raking light**: one low light, in bed axes or relative to the camera (`from: "left"`), to make relief readable.
- **Paint**: support, seam, fuzzy skin and texture paint drawn as tints, with a legend of facets and areas.
- **Section**: cut the plate with a plane, as the 3D view's section view does; cut faces drawn, areas reported.
- **Texture preview**: a texture drawn as its bake would make it, before baking, changing nothing.
- **Compare**: an earlier picture beside a new one, with what changed.
- **Relief, detail and triangle size**: colour a baked surface by how far it moved, by its sharp edges, or by triangle
  size, with a per-height summary that finds bands of coarser triangles.

**3MF**: a part's UV map is now saved in the project, so a Model UV texture layer survives a save and reopen; offered
upstream as OrcaSlicer/OrcaSlicer#16823.

**Fixed**

- A texture bake that ran long ignored the call's time limit: every stage of the bake now checks for a stop, so it ends
  within a second of the limit (or a quit from the app's window).
- The macOS dependency build failed once upstream moved its wxWidgets patches into its own fork.
- The Windows build failed on a function named like a Windows macro.
- The texture panel's Unjoin left the other islands of a group joined when the lowest one was unjoined.
